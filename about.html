About TEIOF
International Impex

TEIOF Impex is a premier global enterprise specializing in the export of superior agro-based products. We bridge the gap between traditional agricultural excellence and the dynamic requirements of the modern global market.

Our ISO 22000:2018, GVCS, and HALAL certifications ensure that every product delivered meets stringent international food safety protocols. We leverage state-of-the-art processing and logistics to maintain the purity and nutritional value of our diverse portfolio.

With registrations including USFDA, FFRMS, and GMP, we stand as a beacon of reliability and innovation in the industry, dedicated to sustainable trade practices and long-term partnerships worldwide.
